Doi chieu Cloud Vision (nang cao)

Doc lai rieng 2 truong so khung / so may bang Google Cloud Vision (mot AI khac, doc lap voi Gemini) de phat hien khi Gemini doc SAI ma tu tin (khong tu bao "can kiem tra"). Neu 2 ben doc khac nhau, he thong se danh dau truong do va ghi ca 2 ket qua vao tin nhan tra loi NVKD de doi chieu. Dung lai Service Account JSON da dien o tren - can vao Google Cloud Console bat Cloud Vision API cho cung project. Cloud Vision co free tier 1000 don vi/thang, sau do tra phi rieng (ngoai chi phi Gemini).

NVKD & han muc OCR

Thang hien tai: -

Them NVKD - Cach 1: tim theo so dien thoai (khuyen nghi)

Chi dung duoc khi da ket noi Zalo o tab dau tien. He thong se tu tra ra Zalo ID cua NVKD tu so dien thoai, Admin khong can biet/nhap ID thu cong.

Them NVKD - Cach 2: nhap Zalo ID truc tiep (nang cao)

Chi dung khi khong tim duoc bang so dien thoai. Co the lay Zalo ID tu tab "Nhat ky OCR" sau khi NVKD da tung nhan tin/gui anh cho Zalo Admin.

Them nhom Zalo - ca nhom dung chung 1 han muc

Ai trong nhom gui anh vao cung duoc doc OCR va tra loi trong nhom, dung chung 1 han muc cua ca nhom (coi nhom nhu 1 "NVKD"). Nhom se TU DONG xuat hien o day ngay sau khi co ai do trong nhom nhan tin/gui anh cho tai khoan Zalo Admin lan dau - khong can dong bo hay tai lai danh sach thu cong. Neu can them ngay 1 nhom chua ai nhan tin, dung Cach 2 o tren (nhap ID nhom + tick "La nhom").
